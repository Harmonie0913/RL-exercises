I compared DQN, RND-DQN, and Ensemble-DQN on LunarLander-v3. 
Ensemble-DQN uses disagreement between several Q-networks as an uncertainty-based exploration signal.

The results show that Ensemble-DQN starts with much worse performance than DQN and RND-DQN. 
This is likely because the ensemble uncertainty is high at the beginning, which leads to more exploratory and unstable actions.

However, Ensemble-DQN improves steadily over training. 
Compared to RND-DQN, its learning curve is smoother and shows a more consistent upward trend. 
RND-DQN reaches good performance at some points, but its curve fluctuates more strongly.

At the final evaluation point, Ensemble-DQN achieves a higher mean return than RND-DQN. 
However, the uncertainty regions overlap, so this result should not be interpreted as a clear win. 
Overall, Ensemble-DQN seems to provide a stable exploration signal, but more seeds or longer training would be needed for a stronger conclusion.
