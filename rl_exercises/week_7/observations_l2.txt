The snapshots show the visited LunarLander states at 10k, 50k, and 100k steps.
The x-axis is state[0], the y-axis is state[1], and the color shows the raw NovelD intrinsic reward.

At 10k steps, exploration is still limited and the intrinsic reward is high.
At 50k steps, the agent visits a wider region, while the intrinsic reward decreases.
At 100k steps, the explored region is larger, but most intrinsic rewards are close to zero.

This behavior is expected: early in training, many states are novel, so NovelD gives stronger exploration rewards.
Later, the RND predictor has learned many visited states, so the novelty signal becomes weaker.
Therefore, the snapshots suggest that NovelD-PPO encourages exploration.
