The results do not show a clear and consistent improvement from RND.

For DQN, RND-DQN is better than DQN at some evaluation points, for example around 25k, 30k, and 45k steps. 
However, the curves are highly unstable and the uncertainty regions strongly overlap. 
Therefore, no reliable positive effect can be concluded.

For PPO, RND-PPO is slightly more stable in the early training phase, but standard PPO clearly performs better later in training. 
PPO continues improving after around 30k steps, while RND-PPO stays at a lower return level.

This suggests that RND is not a very good fit in this setting. 
LunarLander-v3 may not be exploration-hard enough, because epsilon-greedy DQN and entropy-based PPO already provide sufficient exploration. 
The large uncertainty regions suggest high variance, and the RND performance may also be sensitive to hyperparameters such as intrinsic reward weight and predictor update frequency.
