In this run, the DQN agent learned successfully on CartPole-v1. During the first 3000 frames,
the mean reward stayed very low, around 10–20, which indicates almost random behavior. 

After approximately 3000–6000 frames, the reward increased sharply.
This suggests that the replay buffer contained enough useful transitions 
and the Q-network started to learn a meaningful value function.

After around 7000 frames, the mean reward stayed mostly between 170 and 230. 

The second increase after around 12500 frames may be caused by better replay buffer contents.
As the agent improved, the buffer contained more transitions from longer and better episodes. 
These samples helped the Q-network improve further.
