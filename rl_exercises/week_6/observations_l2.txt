I implemented PPO with GAE, the clipped surrogate objective, value-function loss, entropy regularization, mini-batch updates, and multiple optimization epochs.

I also added two implementation enhancements: advantage normalization and gradient clipping. Advantage normalization helps stabilize the scale of the policy update, while gradient clipping prevents very large parameter updates.

On LunarLander-v3, PPO performed clearly better than the actor-critic baseline. The enhanced PPO agent improved more steadily and reached much higher returns than actor-critic. Compared with vanilla PPO, the enhanced version was also more stable and learned faster.

However, PPO did not fully solve the environment within the given training budget, since the final return was not consistently high and positive.
