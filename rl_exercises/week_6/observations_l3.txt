I implemented Soft Actor-Critic for continuous control and also implemented a simplified continuous PPO baseline with a Gaussian policy.

On Pendulum-v1, SAC learned much faster and reached much higher returns than continuous PPO. SAC improved from around -1400 to approximately -100, while continuous PPO stayed around -1500 and did not show clear improvement.

This supports the main claim that SAC is sample-efficient for continuous control. A likely reason is that SAC is off-policy and can reuse transitions from the replay buffer, while PPO is on-policy and discards old data after each update.

However, the PPO baseline was a simplified implementation using a Gaussian policy with clipped actions, and the hyperparameters were not extensively tuned. Therefore, the result should not be interpreted as PPO generally failing on continuous control.
It only shows that, in my implementation and training budget, SAC performed much better.
