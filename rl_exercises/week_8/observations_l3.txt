The experiment shows a statistical precipice effect:

A simple overall mean or overall p-value suggests that REINFORCE is significantly better than DQN. 

However, the per-environment results show a more nuanced picture:

Per-environment tests:
              env                     test  ...   p_value  significant
0     CartPole-v1  paired permutation test  ...  0.065193        False
1      Acrobot-v1  paired permutation test  ...  1.000000        False
2  MountainCar-v0  paired permutation test  ...  1.000000        False

REINFORCE clearly performs better on CartPole, there is no clear advantage on Acrobot, and both algorithms fail on MountainCar. 
Different metrics also lead to different interpretations: 

Aggregate metrics:
   algorithm   n      mean  ...  median_bootstrap_ci_high  iqm_bootstrap_ci_low  iqm_bootstrap_ci_high
0        DQN  15  0.127168  ...                  0.226636              0.039539               0.201818
1  REINFORCE  15  0.306729  ...                  0.656437              0.023043               0.526232

the mean favors REINFORCE, while the median is less supportive because REINFORCE has high variance across environments. 
Therefore, relying on one aggregate score would be misleading.

The probability of improvement shows that REINFORCE does not consistently dominate DQN. 

Probability of improvement:
              env          comparison  n_pairs  probability_of_improvement  mean_difference_reinforce_minus_dqn
0     CartPole-v1  P(REINFORCE > DQN)        5                         1.0                             0.473147
1      Acrobot-v1  P(REINFORCE > DQN)        5                         0.3                             0.065534
2  MountainCar-v0  P(REINFORCE > DQN)        5                         0.5                             0.000000
3             ALL  P(REINFORCE > DQN)       15                         0.6                             0.179560

REINFORCE wins all paired runs on CartPole, but not on Acrobot, and both algorithms perform equally poorly on MountainCar. 
Therefore, the overall advantage of REINFORCE is mainly driven by CartPole rather than by consistent improvement across all environments.
