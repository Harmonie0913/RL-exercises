Using AUC over the evaluation curve as the per-seed aggregate metric, REINFORCE achieved a much higher mean AUC than DQN. 
The paired permutation test gave p = 0.0019998, which is below alpha = 0.05. Therefore, the difference is statistically significant, and REINFORCE performed significantly better than DQN in this experiment.
However, REINFORCE also has larger variance across seeds, as shown by the wider boxplot and wider confidence interval.
